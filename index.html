            Work out due dates from a date you already know

            Child immunisation — from the birth date

            Enter the child's birth date to add four planning visits: 6 weeks, 10 weeks, 14 weeks, and 9 months after birth. These are standard planning intervals — check the child's immunisation card and the local health centre for the exact dates.

            Antenatal — from the expected delivery date

            Enter the expected delivery date to add two check-in visits: 4 weeks before and 1 week before that date. These are suggestions only — change or delete them, and confirm dates with the health centre.

              About this tool

              ASHA stands for Accredited Social Health Activist — a community health worker under India's National Health Mission. The programme began in 2005 under the National Rural Health Mission, and the usual norm is one ASHA per 1,000 people in rural areas. This planner does not give medical advice, name medicines, or set doses. It only helps a health worker keep track of who to visit, when, and what to check. For any health question, the worker should follow the guidance of the local health centre.

              Source: National Health Mission / Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) ASHA guidelines — see the project README for the exact references.
